Shipping & Return Policy

1. Global Freight & Dispatch

Heavy machinery is packed in export-grade, fumigated wooden crates with moisture-barrier wrapping. Dispatches occur via Mundra Port or Kandla Port for overseas shipments, or registered road transit carriers across India.

2. Pre-Dispatch Testing

Every machine undergoes a mandatory 48-hour continuous test run at our Rajkot facility prior to dispatch. Clients receive video test reports or are invited for in-person sign-off.

3. Return Policy

Due to custom engineering and industrial build specifications, machinery returns are not permitted once shipped. Any operational discrepancies are resolved through on-site engineer dispatch and replacement parts under the 1-year warranty framework.
