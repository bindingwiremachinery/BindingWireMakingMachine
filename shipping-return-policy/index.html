Shipping, Warranty & Return Policy

1. Domestic & International Shipping

Domestic shipments across India are handled via dedicated heavy-freight transport lines. International exports are packaged in heavy wooden sea-worthy crates and dispatched via Mundra Port or Kandla Port, Gujarat.

2. 1-Year Machinery Warranty

Every machine sold includes a 1-year warranty covering manufacturing defects in casting, gearboxes, and main shaft bearings. Wear components (e.g., cutter dies, tungsten carbide drawing dies, gripper dies) are consumable items and covered under spare parts support.

3. Industrial Equipment Return Policy

Due to custom engineering and industrial machinery specifications, returns are not permitted once equipment has been dispatched from our Rajkot factory. Replacement parts and engineering support are provided immediately for any warranty-covered defects.
