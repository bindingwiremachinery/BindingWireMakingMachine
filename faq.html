Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common technical, electrical, operational, and global export shipping inquiries.

For wire nails, Low Carbon Mild Steel (MS) or Hard Bright (HB) drawn wire of 8 SWG to 16 SWG is used. For binding wire, low carbon wire rod (typically SAE 1008/1006) drawn down to 18-22 SWG and batch-annealed in an electric furnace is utilized.

Production output ranges from 150 to 550 nails per minute depending on the model (IN1+ to IN6) and target wire gauge. An 8-hour production shift yields between 600 kg and 1,500 kg of finished nails per machine.

We provide vacuum anti-rust barrier wrapping, seaworthy fumigated wooden crating, Bill of Lading (BL), Certificate of Origin (COO), Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and customs clearance support via Mundra/Kandla ports to Kenya, Germany, the UAE, and global destinations.

All machines include a 12-month mechanical warranty covering manufacturing defects. Standard wear items like tungsten cutter dies, gripper dies, and heading punches are stocked in Rajkot for immediate worldwide dispatch.
