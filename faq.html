Technical & Operational FAQs

What raw material is needed for wire nail manufacturing?

The standard raw material is Cold Drawn Hard Bright (HB) Mild Steel Wire with a wire gauge ranging from 8 SWG to 16 SWG depending on the target nail size.

What power supply is required for IN series nail machines?

Our machines run on standard 415V, 3-Phase, 50Hz electrical connections. For international buyers, we customize motors for 220V/380V at 60Hz.

Do you provide operator training and installation?

Yes. We provide complete setup manuals, video tutorials, and remote video guidance. Onsite commissioning by our engineers is also available upon request.

How frequently do cutting dies and gripper tools need maintenance?

Our dies are manufactured from high-grade tungsten carbide and hardened tool steel. With proper lubrication, cutters typically process 50 to 80 tons before requiring re-sharpening on our precision cutter grinder.
