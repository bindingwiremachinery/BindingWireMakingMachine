Frequently Asked Questions

Which nail making machine should I choose?

For nails between 1 to 4 inches, the IN3 Nail Making Machine is the standard choice. For heavy construction nails up to 6 inches, the IN6 is required.

What nail sizes can the IN1+ produce?

The IN1+ produces nails from 0.5 to 2 inches (12 to 50 mm) using 14 to 20 Gauge SWG wire.

What is the production speed of the HS90?

The HS90 High-Speed Nail Machine operates at up to 700 nails per minute, with a daily capacity of up to 1,500 kg.
