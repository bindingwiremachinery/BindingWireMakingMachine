Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do you export machines outside India?

Yes, we are a global exporter. We handle international export crating and support ocean container shipping via Mundra and Kandla ports to Africa, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and the Americas.

2. What is the warranty period on your machines?

All machinery manufactured by Binding Wire Machine carries a comprehensive 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects. We also provide lifetime availability for replacement tooling, dies, and mechanical components.

3. What electrical power supply is required?

Our machines operate on standard 3-phase industrial power (415V, 50Hz for India; customizable to 380V/220V 60Hz for international export destinations).

4. Do you provide onsite installation and training?

Yes. Detailed operation manuals and video tutorials accompany every machine. For full plant setups, our engineers can be dispatched for onsite erection, commissioning, and operator training.
