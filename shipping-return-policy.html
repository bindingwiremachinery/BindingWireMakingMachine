Shipping & Return Policy

Export packaging standards, ocean freight logistics, and equipment warranty terms.

Home / Shipping Policy

1. Export Packaging & Ocean Freight

Every machine prepared for overseas dispatch is coated with industrial anti-corrosion grease, vacuum-sealed in moisture-barrier film, and anchored securely inside heavy-duty, heat-treated wooden crates complying with international ISPM-15 standards.

2. Production Lead Times

Standard IN-series nail making machines (IN1+, IN3, IN4) are dispatched within 10 to 20 working days. Full-scale turnkey plants—including continuous multi-pass wire drawing lines and annealing furnaces—require 30 to 45 working days for fabrication and pre-shipment factory load testing.

3. Capital Equipment Warranty & Tooling Support

All machinery is backed by a 1-year comprehensive mechanical warranty against defects in materials or assembly. Replacement dies, cutters, and grippers are dispatched via express international air courier when required.
