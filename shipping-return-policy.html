Shipping & Return Policy

1. Domestic Dispatch (Pan-India)

Machinery is loaded onto verified commercial freight trucks from our Rajkot plant with transport transit insurance options.

2. International Export Freight

Export orders are packed in fumigated seaworthy wooden crates and shipped via Mundra or Kandla port under FOB, CIF, or CFR terms.

3. Returns & Warranty

Industrial machinery is custom-assembled to client specifications and is not eligible for retail returns. All machinery is protected by a 1-Year Comprehensive Warranty against manufacturing defects.
