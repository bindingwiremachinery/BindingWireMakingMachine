Shipping & Return Policy for Capital Machinery

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Export Packaging & Freight

All industrial machines dispatched for export are coated in moisture-repellent grease, shrink-wrapped in protective plastic barrier film, and bolted inside heavy-duty, fumigated wooden shipping boxes complying with ISPM-15 export regulations.

2. Delivery Lead Times

Standard models (IN1+, IN3, IN4) are dispatched within 10 to 20 working days. Continuous multi-block wire drawing plants and customized binding wire annealing furnaces require 30 to 45 working days for fabrication, electrical wiring, and full load testing.

3. Return Policy on Industrial Equipment

Due to the customized nature and capital scale of heavy industrial equipment, machinery returns are not permitted once goods have cleared customs and left our manufacturing facility. All machines undergo rigorous live video inspection and factory acceptance testing (FAT) with the buyer before dispatch.

4. Replacement of Defective Tooling

In the unlikely event of transit damage or manufacturing defect on specific mechanical components, replacement parts are immediately dispatched via air courier under our 1-Year Comprehensive Warranty.
