Shipping, Export & Commercial Policy

1. Shipping & Logistics
All industrial machinery is quoted on an Ex-Works (EXW) Rajkot facility basis unless FOB, CIF, or CFR terms are explicitly agreed upon in a signed commercial quotation. The buyer is responsible for transit insurance, customs duties, and import taxes in the destination country.

2. Returns & Cancellations
Due to the heavy, customized nature of industrial machinery, returns are not accepted once the machine has left our facility. Orders cancelled after production has commenced will result in the forfeiture of the advance deposit.

3. Warranty & Spare Parts
We provide a standard warranty against manufacturing defects as detailed in the final invoice. Normal wear and tear on cutter dies, drawing blocks, and electrical consumables are excluded. We guarantee long-term availability of spare parts for all our models.
