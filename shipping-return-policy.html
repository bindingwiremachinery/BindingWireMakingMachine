COMMERCIAL LOGISTICS & GUARANTEE

Shipping & Return Policy

Commercial container shipping policies, port terms (FOB/CIF), and machinery guarantee guidelines.

1. Domestic & Global Export Shipping

All machinery orders are processed and dispatched directly from our manufacturing plant at Aji Industrial Area, Phase-2, Rajkot, Gujarat - 360003, India.

  • Domestic Dispatch: Dispatched via verified heavy logistics transport across all Indian states with transit insurance.
  • Export Shipments: Shipped via Mundra Port (INMUN1) or Kandla Port (INIXY1) under Incoterms 2020 (FOB, CIF, or CFR).
  • Packaging Standard: Anti-corrosion vacuum barrier wrap + heavy-duty steel-banded fumigated wooden crates.

2. Dispatch Timelines

Standard models (IN1+ to IN4 Nail Making Machines) typically ship within 7 to 14 business days following advance order confirmation. Complete turnkey binding wire plants and continuous wire drawing lines require 25 to 40 days for fabrication and 48-hour pre-dispatch trial runs.

3. Warranty & Return Policy

Due to the custom-engineered and heavy industrial nature of our machinery, equipment once dispatched is non-returnable for cash refunds. However, every unit is backed by our full warranty:

  • 12-Month Mechanical Warranty: Free replacement for any manufacturing defect on crank bodies, main gears, and shafts.
  • Inspection & Factory Trial: Clients or third-party inspection agencies (SGS, Bureau Veritas) are welcomed for live pre-dispatch testing.
  • Spare Parts Delivery: Immediate worldwide air dispatch via DHL/FedEx for replacement tooling dies and wear components.

4. Port Customs & Documentation

Export documentation provided with each consignment includes: Original Bill of Lading (B/L), Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin (COO), and Fumigation Certificates.
