Continuous Wire Drawing Machine Plants

Engineered for MS, HB, and high-carbon wire rods with water-jacket cooled drawing drums, automatic sensor stops, and power-saving VFD synchronization.

Line Features & Engineering

  • Drum Diameter: 450mm, 550mm, 600mm, 650mm
  • Inlet Wire Size: 5.5 mm up to 10.0 mm wire rod
  • Outlet Wire Range: 1.2 mm to 4.0 mm
  • Number of Blocks: 2 to 8 continuous tandem blocks
  • Drawing Speed: 6 to 14 meters per second

Cooling & Die Technology

Internal water circulation jackets and external forced-air blowers maintain die temperature below critical wear limits during continuous 24-hour operation.
