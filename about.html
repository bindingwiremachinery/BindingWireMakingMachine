Pioneering Industrial Wire & Nail Machinery in Rajkot

Binding Wire Machine is an established industrial engineering and manufacturing enterprise based in the Aji Industrial Area of Rajkot, Gujarat. We specialize in designing, engineering, and exporting high-capacity machinery for wire drawing, nail manufacturing, and binding wire annealing.

Our core philosophy is built upon heavy-duty casting, precision CNC tooling, and minimal operator downtime. Over decades of dedicated development, our IN-series automatic nail machines and multi-block continuous wire drawing plants have gained international acclaim across India, Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa, Germany, Bangladesh, and the Middle East.

"Our mission is to empower global manufacturers with unyielding machinery that scales production seamlessly, reliably, and profitably."
  • Quality Assurance: Multi-stage ultrasonic and dimensional die inspections.
  • Continuous Innovation: High RPM crank mechanics with automated lubrication.
  • Global Support: Guaranteed lifetime availability of replacement dies, cutters, and grippers.
