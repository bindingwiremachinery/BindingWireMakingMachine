About Our Manufacturing Facility

Engineering durable, high-capacity machinery in Rajkot for domestic and international markets.

Two Decades of Mechanical Excellence

Headquartered in Rajkot's prominent Aji Industrial Area, Binding Wire Machine specializes in heavy-duty automatic wire nail making machines, high-speed multi-die wire drawing systems, and full-plant binding wire equipment.

Every machine is built using premium alloy castings, centralized high-pressure lubrication, and hardened tungsten carbide tooling to guarantee round-the-clock reliability and minimal operational downtime.
