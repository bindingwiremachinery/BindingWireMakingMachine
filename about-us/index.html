Pioneering Industrial Manufacturing

Indian Nail Machine is a premier engineering corporation based in Rajkot, Gujarat. We specialize in the design, development, and manufacturing of heavy-duty machinery for the wire and nail industry.

Our core philosophy revolves around delivering robust, high-yielding, and cost-effective production solutions. Over the years, we have mastered the metallurgy and kinematics required to produce machines that run continuously with minimal maintenance. Our IN-series nail making machines and wire drawing plants are recognized for their precision and longevity.

  • Manufacturing Focus: High-speed IN-Series wire nail making machines, wire drawing lines, and binding wire setups.
  • Engineering Capability: In-house CNC machining for heavy-duty crank structures and centralized lubrication systems.
  • Location: Aji Industrial Area, Rajkot, Gujarat – India's premier engineering hub.
