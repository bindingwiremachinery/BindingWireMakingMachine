CONTRACTUAL GUIDELINES

Terms of Service & Sale

Commercial sales terms, quotation validity, electrical specifications, and international export policies for Binding Wire Machine.

Home / Terms of Service

1. Quotation & Proforma Invoice Validity

All pricing published across our website represents baseline factory estimates in Indian Rupees (INR) or US Dollars (USD). Binding sales agreements are formalized exclusively through numbered Proforma Invoices (PI) signed by our sales engineers. Because of fluctuations in global steel rod commodities, motor prices, and international ocean freight rates, formal quotations carry a fixed validity period of 30 calendar days from issuance.

2. Technical & Electrical Configurations

Unless customized prior to contract confirmation, all equipment (including IN-series nail machines, continuous wire drawing lines, and annealing furnaces) is engineered to operate on a 415V, 3-Phase, 50Hz AC industrial power grid. Clients operating on alternative electrical configurations (such as 220V 3-Phase or 380V/440V 60Hz common in parts of North America, Saudi Arabia, UAE, or the Philippines) must specify these requirements during initial consultation to ensure proper motor and VFD control panel winding.

3. Commercial Payment Terms & Production Scheduling

Standard international export terms require a confirmed advance deposit (standard 30% to 50% via Telegraphic Transfer [T/T] or Confirmed Irrevocable Letter of Credit [L/C] at sight) prior to initiating factory assembly. The remaining balance is payable upon successful live video Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) and transmission of certified Bill of Lading (B/L) documentation.

4. Commissioning, Tooling & Spare Parts Support

Standard machines are supplied with an initial set of cutter dies, gripper dies, and pin header punches. Replacement dies and specialized tooling are guaranteed to remain available for direct factory dispatch throughout the operational lifetime of the equipment. For complete turnkey plant installations, engineering deployment for onsite setup and operator training is available under mutually agreed travel and boarding terms.

5. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

All commercial contracts, warranty agreements, and export transactions executed by Binding Wire Machine are governed by the laws of India and remain subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Rajkot, Gujarat, India.
