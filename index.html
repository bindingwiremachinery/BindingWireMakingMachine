Frequently Asked Questions

Which nail making machine should I choose?

Machine selection depends entirely on your required nail length and wire thickness. For nails between 1 to 4 inches, the IN3 Nail Making Machine is the standard choice. For heavy construction nails up to 6 inches, the IN6 is required.

What nail sizes can the IN1+ produce?

The IN1+ produces nails from 0.5 to 2 inches (12 to 50 mm) using 14 to 20 Gauge SWG (0.9 to 2.0 mm) wire.

What is the production speed of the HS90?

The HS90 High-Speed Nail Machine operates at up to 700 nails per minute, with a daily capacity of up to 1,500 kg.

What is the price of the IN3 machine?

The IN3 Nail Making Machine is priced at ₹4,20,000 INR (subject to final commercial quotation terms). Please contact our team for current export pricing.

What is the wire diameter reduction of the 6 Stage Wire Draw Machine?

The 6 Stage Wire Draw Machine reduces wire diameter from 5.5 mm down to 2.5 mm, utilizing a 125 HP motor.

Are electrical accessories included with the wire drawing machines?

No. The 6 Stage and 8 Stage Wire Draw Machines are priced without electricals and accessories. These must be specified and quoted separately.
