HOW IT WORKS

The Turnkey Wire Nail & Binding Wire Production Cycle

From raw steel wire rod to polished, packed nails and annealed binding wire ready for container export.

Plan Your Factory Setup
1

STAGE 01: WIRE DRAWING REDUCTION

Raw 5.5mm wire coils are pulled through continuous tungsten carbide dies to achieve specific nail or binding wire gauges (8 to 20 SWG).

2

STAGE 02: HIGH-SPEED NAIL FORMING

Drawn wire enters the automatic nail making machine for continuous gripping, heading, point cutting, and ejection at up to 550 pcs/min.

3

STAGE 03: POLISHING & ANNEALING

Nails are polished with sawdust in tumbling barrels; binding wire coils undergo controlled electric pit furnace annealing for optimal ductility.

4

STAGE 04: PACKAGING & EXPORT

Finished products are weighed into 1kg, 5kg, or 25kg bundles and boxed for domestic distribution or global container export.
