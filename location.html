Rajkot Factory & Global Logistics

Conveniently situated in Gujarat's premier engineering corridor with immediate highway and port connectivity.

Strategic Location in Aji Industrial Area

Rajkot is globally acknowledged as the capital of casting, forging, and heavy machine tool engineering in India. Operating from Aji Industrial Area Phase-2, we utilize precision foundry castings, induction heat-treatment facilities, and skilled metallurgy experts.

Our plant is directly connected via national highways to major international container ports (Mundra, Kandla, and Nhava Sheva / Mumbai), enabling rapid 20ft and 40ft container dispatch across global shipping routes.
