Supplying Industrial Machinery to Mumbai Manufacturers

While our state-of-the-art manufacturing plant is based in Rajkot, Gujarat, Binding Wire Machine regularly supplies heavy-duty nail and wire processing equipment to industrial buyers in Mumbai, Maharashtra.

Logistics to Maharashtra

We facilitate direct road-transport loading from our Aji GIDC facility. Transit time to Mumbai industrial zones (such as Bhiwandi or Navi Mumbai) is typically 24-48 hours post-dispatch. Buyers can arrange their own logistics or request a CFR quotation inclusive of transport.
