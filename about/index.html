About Our Enterprise

Engineered for durability, speed, and continuous heavy-duty industrial operation.

Pioneering Industrial Manufacturing Since Inception

Under the leadership of proprietor Yasin Ahamed Sandhi, our company designs, manufactures, and exports industrial-grade wire nail making machinery, continuous wire drawing lines, and turnkey binding wire plants across 25+ countries worldwide.
