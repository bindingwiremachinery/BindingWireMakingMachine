IN Series (IN1+, IN3, IN4, IN6)

Our automatic high-speed wire nail making machines are designed to deliver maximum productivity with minimal maintenance. Built with a rigid cast iron body, hardened alloy steel gripper dies, and a centralized automatic oil lubrication system, these units ensure uniform nail head formation, clean diamond point cuts, and smooth shank finishes.

Key Engineering Features

  • Heavy-duty crank structure
  • Centralized automatic lubrication
  • Tungsten carbide cutter dies
  • Low operational noise and vibration
  • Precision wire feeder mechanism
  • Export standard crating & electricals
